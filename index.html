THE ESSENTIALS

Structure, without the noise.

Plan, read, reflect

Set a page target and session duration, then start. One short buzzer marks the start; one marks the finish. There are no recurring alarms, progress check-ins, time announcements, popups, or behind/ahead warnings. Page targets are guides, not pass/fail thresholds.

At the finish

The session ends automatically with one buzzer. Enter how many pages you read, including partial pages. The result is actual pages divided by planned pages × 100. It may exceed 100% and is not a focus or learning score. There is no penalty, backlog, or automatic increase to your next target. Your next plan is yours to set.

Pause, finish, or reset

Pause session freezes the remaining time. Resuming is silent and moves the finish time by the length of the break. Finish early ends the session with its finish buzzer and lets you log pages. Reset & edit plan cancels the timer silently and returns to editable pages and minutes, retaining the previous values.

Space now pauses or resumes an active session. There is no interval to restart. It works across this page and in display windows, including focused controls; holding it does not repeatedly toggle. Enter activates focused buttons. Modified shortcuts are left alone. F opens the floating display outside fields or dialogs; Esc leaves focus view or closes a dialog.

Sound and reliability

The buzzer is synthesized in the browser. Volume zero mutes it. Pausing, resuming, entering results, and resetting do not play sounds. The end sound is scheduled with Web Audio while audio is available, and the timer checks actual clock time rather than counting browser ticks. Changes to your device clock can change the apparent remaining time.

Browsers and operating systems may suspend background pages or audio, particularly when an iPhone is locked or a Mac sleeps. This is not a dependable lock-screen alarm. Keep the page open, visible and your device awake. A late return shows the completed session; its finish sound may be late or unavailable. There is no repeating alarm or backlog of beeps.

Keep screen awake requests a screen wake lock while the page is visible and a session is running. The browser may reject or release it. It cannot override a manual lock or guarantee background audio.

Refresh and privacy

Preferences are saved locally. The current session and its result are saved for reloads in the same tab when browser storage is available; closing the tab may remove them. A running session continues against its original deadline after a refresh. The browser may require you to click Enable finish buzzer to restore audio. A reload never replays the start sound. Stored settings and results do not automatically move between devices or from a local HTML file to a hosted site.

This is one self-contained HTML file. No remote scripts, analytics, trackers, accounts, or uploads. Your entered pages, preferences, and imported font remain in this browser. Clearing browser data removes saved information. Hosting serves the app, not your reading data.

Floating display

The floating timer uses an always-on-top window where Document Picture-in-Picture is supported; otherwise it opens an ordinary window. Extra displays mirror the same session and do not duplicate the buzzer. Keep the main tab open. Your appearance controls and local font import remain available.
